Conrad's Game of Life

Every living cell is a virtue. Push for space. Reach temperance.

Harmony 0%

Seed virtues, then let them seek the right amount of space.

Controls

Click a cell to place the selected virtue. Right-click to erase. Too little space: push (swap or shove a block). Too much space: step toward other virtues until every cell has the right amount.

Virtues

Select one, then paint. Ideal space is empty Moore neighbors.
